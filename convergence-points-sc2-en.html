Convergent Points – Terran

StarCraft II: Legacy of the Void · summary of the TerranCraft article

What they are

Benchmarks for how many production buildings (Barracks‑Factory‑Starport) you should have for each number of mining bases, so you spend your resources efficiently.

  • Different builds end up converging on the same production setup.
  • They tell you whether your build is on track: too much production means idle buildings; too little means resources bank up.
  • They assume constant production from every building, including Command Centers.
  • Terran only, because of Terran's linear production. Zerg has no convergent point (it doesn't add production as it takes bases). Protoss is mentioned but not covered.

Unsaturated

Minerals pile up, so you can afford to expand.

Saturated

You spend everything and can't afford another Command Center.

1 base

Not a true convergent point, since nobody "converges" to one base. Close‑to‑saturation examples: 3‑Rax Reaper and some 1‑1‑1 variants with a heavily delayed CC.

2 bases: the most important point 3‑1‑1

Most builds converge here, and it marks the end of the build order.

3 bases 5‑1‑1

The main three‑base checkpoint. After it you have flexibility (stage 2), with matchup‑ and mirror‑specific adjustments.

Rule of thumb: no more than 12 production buildings in total, assuming they all produce at once.

What to expect by matchup

TvT

Expect a second Factory for tanks: 5‑2‑1. One Barracks with a Tech Lab handles Stim and Combat Shield; the rest get Reactors.

TvP

2‑1‑1 is common; a third Barracks gets you to 3‑1‑1. With Liberators and Medivacs, go 5‑1‑2, then toward 8‑1‑2. In the 5‑1‑1: 3 Tech Labs and 2 Reactors.

TvZ

Standard Reaper expand into a 1‑1‑1 variant with an early third CC. In the 5‑1‑1: 1 Tech Lab and 4 Reactors.

Mech / Air

Mech: 1‑3‑1, then 1‑5‑1. Air: 1‑1‑3, then 1‑1‑5, adding more Starports as the game goes on.

What to check in game

  • Production vs bases: compare your Rax/Fact/Port count with the benchmark for the bases you're mining from.
  • Resources: spare minerals mean you can add production or expand. Idle buildings mean too much production.
  • Engineering Bays: 2 for an unsaturated macro game, or 1 if you plan to stay on two bases.
  • Add‑ons: little effect on the convergent point, except when you have extra Factories.
  • Late game (LotV): your main may mine out while your third is still starting, so you may stay at 5‑1‑1, 5‑2‑1 or 5‑1‑2 longer before reaching 11–12 buildings.

The article gives no specific timings for each point. The only time mentioned is 10:00, as an example of when players usually have at least three mining bases. The article is from 2016 (early LotV): the benchmarks are still useful, but current compositions and timings may differ.
