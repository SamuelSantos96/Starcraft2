Most Common Terran Build Orders

StarCraft II · TvT, TvP and TvZ · November 2026

Shared base across all three matchups

Reaper Fast Expand → 1‑1‑1

From there, each matchup branches off.

TvT

Reaper/Hellion → Marine‑Tank (‑Raven) standard

The most solid and common opening, transitioning into Marine‑Tank and Raven.

Cyclone opening mech or bio

Still seen in 2026 replays, e.g. in mech TvT.

Aggressive alternatives

  • 1‑1‑1 with Banshee
  • Tank push
  • Proxy Reaper (less common)

Convergent point: expect a 2nd Factory for tanks, 5‑2‑1.

TvP

Reaper FE → bio standard

  • Reaper scouts for proxies.
  • The SCV that builds the 2nd depot builds the Bunker.
  • Factory after the Orbital; 2nd gas after the Bunker.
  • Scout with Reaper + Hellion.

2‑1‑1 Marine‑Medivac timing pressure

Classic early pressure: 2 Medivacs carrying 15–16 Marines move across the map around 4:45, transitioning into a tank push.

Other options

  • Cloak Banshee → two‑base, five‑Barracks all‑in
  • 2 Rax + Marauders (pressure against a fast expand)

TvZ

Reaper FE → Reactor Hellions → 1‑1‑1 → 4M standard

4M = Marine, Marauder, Medivac, Mine. The standard style in the matchup.

Hellion‑Banshee HeroMarine 2026

  • Basic Reaper FE.
  • Reaper scouts the third base and heads back around 3:20.
  • Move out with 2 Hellions + Reaper.
  • Then: more CCs, 2nd Factory for tanks, upgrades, Fusion Core, Ghosts.

Variants

  • Reactor Hellion + Battlecruiser (BC when the Fusion Core finishes)
  • Mech
  • Fast 3 CC (uThermal)

If you spot early Roaches

Cut Hellion/Marine production, focus on Mines/Marauders, and add 1–2 extra Bunkers.

Patch warning

5.0.16 (June 2026) cut starting workers from 12 to 8. 5.0.17 (October 7, 2026) restored 12. Guides published over the summer may use 8‑worker timings, so check the patch version before copying supply counts.
